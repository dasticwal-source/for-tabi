A birthday letter

Tabi,

I don't know what place I hold in your memories now. I only know that you once held an entire world inside mine.

What we had existed through screens, cities and years without ever becoming less real to me. You were my first love—the person who made ordinary nights feel like a life I wanted to keep.

This isn't here to ask you for an answer, or to make your day carry the weight of my sadness. Today belongs to you. I only wanted to leave something beautiful at its door.

May twenty-one bring you peace, courage, laughter, and the kind of love that never asks you to become smaller. Wherever life takes us, I will remain grateful that somewhere in a game full of strangers, I found you.

Happy birthday, Tabi.
— Rahul
